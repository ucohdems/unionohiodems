About Our Meetings

When do you meet?

The Union County Democratic Party holds a monthly membership meeting on the last Tuesday of each month. Doors open at 6:00 PM for the social hour; the formal meeting begins at 7:00 PM and typically wraps up by 8:30 PM.

Occasional special events — candidate forums, watch parties, canvassing kickoffs — are announced in our monthly newsletter and posted here as they're scheduled.

Who can come?

All meetings are open to the public. You don’t need to be a registered Democrat, a dues-paying member, or a Union County resident to attend. Bring a friend, bring a neighbor, bring a curious Republican — all are welcome.

If you’d like to get involved or have a question before showing up, drop us a line at ucohdems@gmail.com.

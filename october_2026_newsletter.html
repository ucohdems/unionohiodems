📢 Early Voting Is Open!

Don't wait for Election Day. Cast your ballot now at the Union County Board of Elections.

📍 Board of Elections Early Voting

Location: 835 E. 5th Street, Marysville, OH 43040

Hours: Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Early voting runs through November 2nd. Bring a valid photo ID. Extended hours may apply closer to Election Day — check with the BOE for the latest schedule.

Every early vote locks in a Democratic ballot before Election Day logistics can interfere.

✋ Actions Needed

Deadline: Oct 20

🔍 Become a Poll Observer

The Democratic Party is recruiting poll observers to be present at polling locations on Election Day. This is a critical role in ensuring election integrity and fairness.

  • Commit to as little or as much of the day as you're able
  • Paperwork must be submitted before October 20 for the initial BOE list (due October 23)
  • Amended list deadline: November 2 by 4:00 PM

Interested? Email us and we'll get you the paperwork and details.

Before Oct 24

🍬 Scarysville Candy Drive

We need your help stocking up for Scarysville on October 24! We welcome both traditional candy and food-friendly treat items for the little ghouls and goblins in our community.

We advocate for all families, including food-insecure ones. Items like the following are fantastic additions alongside candy:

  • Pretzels, chips, or crackers
  • Fruit snacks or raisins
  • Individual popcorn packs
  • Granola bars or rice cakes
  • Any individually wrapped non-candy snack

Drop off donations at HQ before October 24. Thank you!

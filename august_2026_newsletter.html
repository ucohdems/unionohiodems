🚨 We Need You

⏰ Urgent

Help Our Haitian Neighbors in Springfield

Springfield is now part of Ohio's 4th Congressional District — which means these are our neighbors, and this is our fight too.

Haitian community members in Springfield are being forced to wear ankle monitors and have just 7 days from receiving them to file an appeal. Volunteer organizers are urgently needed to help community members fill out these critical forms — and time is running out.

Volunteers must be able to:

  • Travel to Springfield in person with a laptop
  • Commit to at least one 3-hour shift per week for the next 3 weeks
  • Be available between 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM

If your heart is called to help, please reach out directly to get started. Every form filed is a fight for someone's freedom.

Share Your Voice: Voting Rights Video Campaign

The Allison Russo for Secretary of State campaign is building a powerful social media campaign about voting rights — and they need your story.

Frank Sprague has called for eliminating drop boxes and mail-in voting. The campaign is collecting short video submissions from Ohioans explaining why they use these voting methods and what losing them would mean for their community. As Rural Caucus members, our voices matter enormously in this conversation.

Interested? Fill out the form below and the campaign will reach out to work with you. They can even help you film in person if needed — so don't let camera shyness stop you!
